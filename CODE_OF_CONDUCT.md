# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the AirBridge project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, technical expertise, or identity.

## Expected Standards

We expect all participants to:
- Demonstrate empathy and respect toward others.
- Provide and gracefully accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community and project quality.
- Respect differing perspectives and technical approaches.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Disruptive speech or non-constructive behavior in discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces, including the repository, issue trackers, and pull requests.
